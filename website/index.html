Privacy

How Sieu-Sv_Library handles account and community data.

Information we store

When you sign in with Google, the service receives your Google account identifier and the basic profile fields you choose to make available, such as email verification status, display name and avatar. The library stores your username, display name, avatar, bio, account creation date and username-change history.

Content and activity

Scripts you publish, their descriptions and thumbnails are stored to display them in the public library. Like and dislike votes are stored with the relevant account so the library can prevent duplicate votes and show your current vote.

Service providers

The site is hosted through Cloudflare Workers and uses Cloudflare D1 for application data. Google is used for sign-in. GitHub hosts the source repository and license text. These providers may process technical request data under their own policies.

Public information

Your username, display name, avatar, bio and public scripts may be visible to other visitors. Do not put private contact details or sensitive information in your public profile or scripts.

Retention and choices

Account and published-content data are kept while needed to operate the service. You can edit your profile and may request account or content removal from the repository owner. Some records, such as security and abuse-prevention records, may be retained when necessary.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the repository owner through the GitHub repository linked below.

About

Sieu-Sv_Library

A community library for sharing and discovering scripts and projects.

Project

Sieu-Sv_Library is the web library connected to the SieuSV-Storage repository. Visitors can browse public scripts, inspect source code, open creator profiles and vote on community scripts.

The repository is maintained at minhReal/SieuSV-Storage.

Credits

Project credits and plugin information are documented in the repository README and LICENSE.

Community

Rules

Keep the library useful and safe for everyone.

  1. Only publish content you created or are allowed to redistribute.
  2. Do not upload malware, credential theft, hidden data collection or destructive scripts.
  3. Do not impersonate another creator or misrepresent ownership.
  4. Do not include private information, passwords, tokens or personal contact details in public scripts or profiles.
  5. Do not spam uploads, votes, comments or other service endpoints.
  6. Do not use the library to harass people, evade platform safeguards or violate applicable laws or platform rules.
  7. Review scripts before running them. The source library does not guarantee that community submissions are trustworthy.

Content that breaks these rules may be hidden or removed.
